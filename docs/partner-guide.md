# Formavo — Partner Walkthrough

*A guided first week with Formavo: youth soccer club management built around the
touchline, not the office.*

Formavo runs a youth club's season end to end — teams, rosters, schedules,
parent communication — but its heart is the **live match console**: a coach runs
the whole game from their phone (lineup, subs, goals, formation switches), and
everything downstream builds itself: live score pushes to parents, shareable
recaps, player minutes, season stats, and club-level reports.

---

## 1. Getting in

1. Accept the **TestFlight invite** (email) on an iPhone → install Formavo.
2. Open the app and **create an account** with the same email your invite was
   sent to — any pending team invites attach to your account automatically on
   sign-up.
3. You'll land with access to **U13 Tigers (Demo)** in **Formavo FC (Demo)** —
   a fictional team with a season of real-looking data. Nothing in it is real,
   so tap, edit, and break anything you like.

## 2. Ten-minute tour of the demo

**Home tab** — the week's schedule, a "next up" hero with countdown and RSVP
glance, weather for the upcoming match, and the last result.

**Teams tab → U13 Tigers (Demo):**

- Open a **completed match** (e.g. vs Cape Rovers) → tap **📊 Recap**: scoreline,
  goal timeline, shot map, minutes per player, and a Share button. Notice
  *"Presented by Harbourview Hardware"* — clubs sell that line to a sponsor.
- Tap the **⭐ Rate** pill on a completed match to see post-match player ratings
  (they feed the development log).
- Open the **Roster**, tap a player → **profile**: season stats, Season Card
  (shareable), Season Report (report card for parents), and **four-corner
  assessments** (Technical / Tactical / Physical / Mental across the season).
- **Stats tab**: team record and leaderboards — try the **League / Cup /
  Friendly** filter chips.

**Club level** — from the Teams screen open the club dashboard:

- **📊 Club Reports**: *Records* (per-competition W-D-L per team), *Equity*
  (playing-time fairness with under-median flags — receipts for the "why isn't
  my kid playing" conversation), *Positions* (who's stuck in one spot), and
  *Adoption* (which coaches actually use the tools).
- **📝 Tryouts**: sessions, quick-add candidates, tap-star ratings,
  keep/maybe/pass shortlists you can share.

## 3. Run a live match (the core demo)

The demo team has upcoming fixtures. Open one, then **Game Day**:

1. **Kick off** — the clock runs; parents with the app get a live match card.
2. **Log a goal**: tap the scorer on the pitch → Goal → optional assist and
   spot on the mini-pitch. Parents get a push with the score *as it happens*.
3. **Make a sub**: tap a bench player, place them on a slot — minutes tracking
   handles rolling subs automatically.
4. **Switch formation mid-game** — players re-slot to their natural positions
   by their position priority.
5. Mistakes are safe: **undo** the last event, or remove any event later
   ("logged by mistake" / "disallowed by referee, with reason").
6. **Full time** → the recap, minutes, stats, and club reports all update
   themselves.

That's the pitch in one sentence: *one coach logging a game feeds every report
a parent, coach, or club administrator will ever ask for.*

## 4. Set up a real team (10 minutes)

1. **Teams → create a team** (age group, season).
2. **Roster → ⇪ Import roster (paste a list)** — paste straight from a
   spreadsheet: `name, number, parent email` per line, any order. Players are
   created and **parent invites go out by email** in one pass.
3. **Matches → ⇪ Import fixtures from league schedule** — paste the league's
   calendar link (ICS) or a CSV; tag the batch (league/cup/friendly) once.
4. Done. Parents accept their invite, see only their kids, and can RSVP.

## 5. What each role sees

| | Coach | Parent | Club staff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schedule + RSVP | full control | RSVP own kids | view |
| Live console | run the game | live score view | view |
| Stats & reports | team-level | own kids | every team |
| Chat & announcements | post + moderate | read + chat | post |

Parents are deliberately read-and-RSVP: they can't touch lineups, events, or
other people's kids — enforced server-side, not just hidden in the UI.

## 6. Honest current limits

- **iOS only today.** Android is the next platform milestone.
- Payments/registration collection isn't built yet (planned for the club tier).
- English only for now.
