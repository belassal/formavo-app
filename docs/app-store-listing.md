## Description
Formavo is youth soccer club management built around the touchline, not the
office.

RUN THE MATCH FROM YOUR PHONE
Kick off, log goals with a tap on the pitch, make subs, switch formation
mid-game. Record voice notes about what you're seeing — minute-stamped,
private to staff. Undo anything.

EVERYTHING DOWNSTREAM BUILDS ITSELF
The moment you hit full time: a shareable recap with goal timeline and shot
map, per-player minutes, season stats, and club reports. One coach logging a
game feeds every report a parent, coach or club administrator will ever ask
for.

PARENTS SEE EXACTLY WHAT THEY SHOULD
Live scores pushed as they happen, the week's schedule, RSVP for their own
kids — and nothing else. Parent access is read-and-RSVP by design, enforced
server-side.

BUILT FOR CLUBS
Playing-time equity reports (set your own fairness threshold), per-competition
records, position exposure, staff with real per-team positions, tryout
shortlists, sponsor placement on every recap.

SET UP IN TEN MINUTES
Paste your roster from a spreadsheet — players created and parent invites
emailed in one pass. Paste your league's schedule link and the fixtures
import themselves.

Formavo is currently onboarding clubs personally. Request your club inside
the app and we'll have you running your first match day the same week.
