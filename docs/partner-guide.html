1 · Getting in

  1. Accept the TestFlight invite (arrives by email) on an iPhone and install Formavo.
  2. Create an account with that same email. Pending team invites attach automatically the moment you sign up.
  3. Open U13 Tigers (Demo) in Formavo FC (Demo) — a fictional team with a full season of data. Nothing in it is real, so tap, edit, and break anything you like.

2 · Ten-minute tour of the demo

Home tab — this week's schedule, a “next up” hero with countdown, RSVP glance and match-day weather, and the last result.

Open a completed match → 📊 Recap Scoreline, goal timeline, shot map, minutes per player, one-tap share.
⭐ Rate on a completed match Post-match player ratings that feed each player's development log.
Roster → tap a player Season stats, shareable Season Card, Season Report for parents, and four-corner assessments (Technical / Tactical / Physical / Mental).
Stats tab Team record and leaderboards — try the League / Cup / Friendly filter chips.

Club level — from the Teams screen, open the club dashboard:

📊 Club Reports Records (per-competition W-D-L per team) · Equity (playing-time fairness with under-median flags — receipts for the “why isn't my kid playing” conversation) · Positions (who's stuck in one spot) · Adoption (which coaches actually use the tools).
📝 Tryouts Sessions, quick-add candidates, tap-star ratings, keep / maybe / pass shortlists you can share.

3 · Run a live match

The demo team has upcoming fixtures. Open one, then Game Day:

  1. Kick off. The clock runs; parents with the app get a live match card.
  2. Log a goal. Tap the scorer on the pitch → Goal → optional assist and the spot on the mini-pitch. Parents get the score pushed as it happens.
  3. Make a sub. Tap a bench player, place them in a slot — minutes tracking handles rolling subs automatically.
  4. Switch formation mid-game. Players re-slot to their natural positions by position priority.
  5. Mistakes are safe. Undo the last event, or remove any event later — “logged by mistake” or “disallowed by referee”, with a reason.
  6. Full time. The recap, minutes, stats and club reports all update themselves.

One coach logging a game feeds every report a parent, coach, or club administrator will ever ask for.

4 · Set up a real team in 10 minutes

  1. Create a team in the Teams tab (age group, season).
  2. Import the roster. Roster → ⇪ Import roster (paste a list). Paste straight from a spreadsheet — name, number, parent email per line, any order. Players are created and parent invites go out by email in one pass.
  3. Import the fixtures. Matches → ⇪ Import fixtures from league schedule. Paste the league's calendar link (ICS) or a CSV; tag the whole batch league / cup / friendly once.
  4. Done. Parents accept their invite, see only their own kids, and can RSVP.

6 · Honest current limits

  • iOS only today. Android is the next platform milestone.
  • Payments / registration collection isn't built yet (planned for the club tier).
  • English only for now.
