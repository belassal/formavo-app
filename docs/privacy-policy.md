Formavo ("we", "us") is a team-management app for youth and amateur football
clubs, operated by [legal entity name, address, contact email]. This policy
explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## Who provides the data

Formavo accounts are created by adults: coaches, club staff, and
parents/guardians. **Children do not create accounts.** Information about
players who are minors is entered by their coach or parent/guardian, who is
responsible for having the authority to provide it.

## Information we collect

**Account information** — name, email address, and password (stored by Firebase
Authentication; we never see your password). Optional profile photo and phone
number.

**Player information (entered by coaches/parents)** — player name, jersey
number, positions, date of birth, optional photo, guardian contact details,
and coach notes.

**Sports activity data** — match schedules, results, goals/assists/cards,
minutes played, training attendance, coach ratings and development notes,
lineups, and RSVP responses.

**Team communication** — chat messages, announcements, and photos shared
within a team.

**Device data** — a push-notification token for your device, and standard
technical logs generated by our service providers.

We do **not** collect precise device location. Venue addresses are entered
manually by coaches.

## How we use it

- To provide the service: schedules, rosters, live match tracking, statistics.
- To send notifications you can control in Settings (goals, schedule changes,
  RSVP reminders, chat, announcements, weekly digests).
- To send invite emails when a coach invites you to a team or club.
- We do **not** sell personal information or use it for third-party
  advertising.

## Who can see what

Team data is visible only to that team's members: coaches/staff see everything
for their team; parents see the team schedule, roster names, results and
statistics, and their own child's detailed profile. Club administrators see
data for teams in their club. Nothing is public.

## Service providers

Data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication,
Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) in [region — currently
us-central1, United States]. Email delivery uses [email provider used by the
Trigger Email extension]. These providers process data on our behalf under
their own data-processing terms.

  contact us at [contact email] to have a child's record corrected or removed.

## Children
