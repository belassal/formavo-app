Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Formavo ("we", "us") is a team-management app for youth and amateur soccer clubs. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions can go to admin@formavo.ca.

The short version

Team data is visible only to that team's members and club staff — nothing is public. We don't sell personal information, show ads, or track you across other apps. Children don't create accounts; their coach or parent enters their information.

Who provides the data

Formavo accounts are created by adults: coaches, club staff and parents/guardians. Children do not create accounts. Information about players who are minors is entered by their coach or parent/guardian, who is responsible for having the authority to provide it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address and password (stored by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password). Optional profile photo and phone number.
  • Player information (entered by coaches or parents) — player name, jersey number, positions, date of birth, optional photo, guardian contact details and coach notes.
  • Sports activity data — match schedules, results, goals, assists, cards, minutes played, training attendance, coach ratings and development notes, lineups and RSVP responses.
  • Team communication — chat messages, announcements and photos shared within a team, and voice notes recorded by coaching staff.
  • Device and usage data — a push-notification token for your device, crash reports, and information about how the app's features are used (for example, which screens are opened), linked to your account ID.

We do not collect your device's location. Venue addresses are entered manually by coaches.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: schedules, rosters, live match tracking, statistics and reports.
  • To send notifications you control in Settings — live scores, schedule changes, RSVP reminders, chat, announcements and weekly digests.
  • To send invite emails when a coach or club invites you.
  • To find and fix crashes and understand which features are used, so we can improve the app.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Who can see what

Team data is visible only to that team's members. Coaches and staff see everything for their team. Parents see the team schedule, roster names, results and statistics, and their own child's detailed profile. Coach notes, ratings and voice notes are visible to staff only. Club administrators see data for teams in their club. These rules are enforced on our servers, not just in the app. Nothing is public unless a team member chooses to share something outside the app (for example, sharing a match recap).

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics and Analytics) stores and processes app data in the United States (us-central1).
  • Email delivery — invite, notification and account emails are sent through a transactional email provider.
  • Maps and weather — when a coach searches for a venue, the search text is sent to OpenStreetMap-based geocoding services (Nominatim, Photon). Match-day weather is fetched from Open-Meteo using the venue's coordinates. No personal information is included in these requests.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own data-processing terms.

Retention and deletion

  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This removes your login, personal profile, team and club memberships and pending invites.
  • Player records are managed by the team's coach. Parents may ask the coach, or email admin@formavo.ca, to have a child's record corrected or removed.
  • We keep team sports records while the team's account remains active.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. Email admin@formavo.ca and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Formavo is not directed at children and accounts are for adults only. Information about minor players is provided and controlled by their coaches and parents/guardians as described above. If you believe a child's information was provided without appropriate authority, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
